01 / Who I am

I grew up in Singapore and went to Raffles Institution, where I started a podcast about failure after losing a student council election. It ended up running for ten episodes and reaching 5,000+ listeners, with politicians, business leaders and athletes as guests, all reached through cold outreach.

National Service put me in a finance office managing a S$3.6M procurement budget. In the evenings I bootstrapped an A-Level Economics tutoring business, growing it from 5 to 40+ students in a year.

Now I'm reading Economics & Management at Hertford College, Oxford. I care most about problems I've seen up close: EstateFlow started after watching my mother navigate my grandmother's estate, and a parliamentary speech on National Service pay started with noticing that allowances weren't enough for servicemen supporting their families.

Based in Oxford & Singapore

02 / Now

Final year at Oxford, and splitting the rest of my time three ways.

Building EstateFlow. In the UK, bereaved families can wait 9 to 12 months for probate before they can touch an estate. EstateFlow is a legaltech platform that releases that liquidity earlier. We won the Oxford FinTech Innovation Sprint and are part of the Oxford Venture Builders pre-accelerator.

Investing. I lead consumer and emerging markets coverage at the Oxford Alpha Fund, and I've just come back from a summer of equity research at Fidelity in Hong Kong covering Chinese consumer, healthcare and internet names.

Getting better at AI. I'm trying to use it seriously every day: for research, for building, and for things like this website.
